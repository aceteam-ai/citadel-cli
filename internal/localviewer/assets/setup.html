Citadel setup for local mode on this computer

Everything here stays on this computer. Citadel makes no network calls of its own and needs no account.

1. Is this computer ready?

    Starts a hidden browser for a few seconds to prove it works.

    2. Connect your agent app

    Clicking Connect adds Citadel to that app's settings file, shown beside the button. Your other settings are kept, and a backup of the old file is saved next to it. That click is your permission.

      Another app?

      Apps Citadel does not know yet (Muse, for example, has no settings file Citadel can write to) can use this entry in their MCP settings. It starts Citadel's local tools only.
